Privacy at Fairyland Cottage

Cookies & third-party services

Here is a clear guide to the services this site uses and how they may interact with your browser.

At a glance

We do not use Google Analytics or advertising pixels. Some features connect to third-party services, which may use cookies or receive technical information when you use them.

Services used on this site

Contact form EmailJS

When you submit the contact form, the details you provide are sent to EmailJS so your message can be delivered.

Payment PayPal

The shop opens PayPal’s hosted checkout. PayPal may use cookies or similar technologies during checkout.

Fonts and icons Google Fonts & Font Awesome

The site requests fonts and icons from these providers so they can be displayed in your browser.

Your choices

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking third-party services may affect the contact form, checkout, or how parts of the site display. This site does not have an on-page cookie preference panel.
